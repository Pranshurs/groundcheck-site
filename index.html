Know when your AI answer isn't in the source.

GroundCheck Pro verifies that a RAG or Q&A answer is actually supported by the document it cites — one call, sub-second on CPU, your data never leaves your infrastructure.

Request API access See the open model

The problem

RAG systems confidently state things their sources never say — a swapped number, a flipped figure, a fact pulled from nowhere. Catching this with a frontier LLM-as-judge is slow, costs money per call, and ships your users' data to a third party. GroundCheck Pro is a small, specialized model that does just this one job.

How it works

curl -s https://api.groundcheck.dev/v1/check \
  -H "X-API-Key: $GROUNDCHECK_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "source": "In Q3, revenue increased 12% year-over-year to $2.1 billion.",
    "answer": "The company's revenue fell 12% in Q3."
  }'

# → { "label": "hallucinated", "p_grounded": 0.01, "latency_ms": 38 }

Add "granular": true to get a verdict per sentence, or POST to /v1/check/batch to score up to 100 answers at once for offline log audits.
