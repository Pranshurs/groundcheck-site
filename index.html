GroundCheck Pro: in development

A commercial version of GroundCheck, the open grounding checker for RAG answers, is being built. It isn't available yet: there's no hosted API, no access keys and no pricing.

Use the open model Get in touch

The problem

RAG systems sometimes state things their sources never say: a swapped number, a flipped figure, a fact pulled from nowhere. GroundCheck checks whether an answer is supported by the source it was given, with a small classifier instead of an LLM judge.

Available today: the open model

GroundCheck v2 is a ModernBERT-base classifier. It's open, and its evaluation can be reproduced: pinned weights, public data builders, committed evaluation reports and a regression gate. All published numbers, their protocol and their limitations are documented in the repository. The code is Apache-2.0 and the weights are MIT; the training data keeps its upstream terms, some of which are non-commercial.

What Pro is meant to add

Sentence-level verdicts

Which part of an answer is unsupported, not just whether.

Batch auditing

Score logged answers in bulk to catch regressions.

Commercial-use data

Training data cleared for commercial use.

These are plans. No Pro benchmark results are published yet.
